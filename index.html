BRING YOUR NUMBERS TOGETHER

Add analytics

Import a dashboard backup or a daily CSV. For a raw export, choose its columns below. Preview and confirm before saving.

Use daily counts, not cumulative or overlapping period totals. Followers are a snapshot. Do not combine a host's all-platform downloads with platform plays.
CSV format and metric definitions

Template columns: date, show, platform, metric, value, episode. Dates must be YYYY-MM-DD. Show is better-smb or moments; platform is spotify, apple or host. Leave episode blank for show totals. Repeat imports replace records with the same date, show, platform, metric and episode.

Listeners cannot be added across days to find unique people. The episode listener view shows listener-days. Consumption percentages are kept in backups but not averaged. Unavailable or privacy-suppressed figures should be omitted, not entered as zero.

For reporting totals from a date range, obtain daily data first. This version cannot safely combine overlapping period exports.
